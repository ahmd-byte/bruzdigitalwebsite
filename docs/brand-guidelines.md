# Brand guidelines

## Approved tagline
**We build tech. You build business.**

Keep capitalization and punctuation as shown.

## Approved color palette
| Name | Hex | Primary use |
|---|---|---|
| Signal Blue | `#163B8C` | Primary actions, strong brand blocks, selected navigation |
| Turquoise | `#16B8C4` | Active system states, data flows, highlights |
| Frost | `#F3F7FB` | Reading surfaces and light panels |
| Ink | `#10243A` | Main text, dark panels, diagrams |
| Orange | `#F49A38` | Limited emphasis and small visual markers |

## Contrast guidance
Calculated sRGB contrast ratios:
- White on Signal Blue: **10.30:1**
- Ink on Frost: **14.61:1**
- Ink on Turquoise: **6.51:1**

Ink on Turquoise clears the 4.5:1 WCAG AA target for normal text. Recheck actual component states and text sizes during implementation. Avoid small white text on Turquoise and avoid using Orange as small text on Frost; use Ink for text on bright fills.

## Typography and voice
- Plus Jakarta Sans is the current site font and remains the baseline while options are reviewed.
- Typography proposals are not approved until selected by the user.
- Use direct, specific language. Avoid unsupported claims, invented metrics, and generic promises.
- Use the tagline consistently. Do not create a second competing slogan without approval.

## Logo and imagery
- Use the existing BRUZ wordmark treatment until a verified logo asset is supplied.
- Preserve verified team portraits and approved project details.
- Label product interface illustrations as illustrations. Do not present them as live product screenshots.
