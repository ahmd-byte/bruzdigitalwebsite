# UX decisions

## Approved or established
- The homepage communicates “We build tech. You build business.” immediately.
- The existing hero uses a 52/48 split between copy and visual content.
- The System Lab sequence explains the architecture, transforms into product UI, then settles; it does not loop.
- Main navigation covers Work, Studio, Products, Team, and Contact.
- Product illustrations are explicitly identified as illustrations.
- Contact routes to the project brief and the confirmed email address.
- Mobile navigation and semantic anchors are part of the existing site.

## Design constraints
- Keep the first screen clear about who Bruz serves and what it builds.
- Make products and client work easy to distinguish.
- Use color with text or shape cues so status does not depend on color alone.
- Provide readable body copy, visible keyboard focus, and reduced-motion behavior.
- Preserve factual content and verified assets.

## Open questions for later design review
- Which typography system best balances modern product identity and established site consistency?
- Which homepage sections should transition from dark surfaces to Frost surfaces?
- What new social card and favicon treatment best fits Signal Blue?
- Which product preview details should be shown at mobile width?
