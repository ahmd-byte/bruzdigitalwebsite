# Brand discovery

## Project
Bruz Digital presents a hybrid studio and product business. It builds custom technology and applied AI for business workflows while developing focused products.

## Approved brand promise
**We build tech. You build business.**

This expresses the working relationship simply: Bruz handles technology; clients focus on business goals.

## Audience and need
The website should help business decision makers understand what Bruz builds, see credible work and products, and start a conversation. Keep technical credibility visible while explaining value in plain language.
