02 / Build path

From field signal to shared action.

The prototype organized the challenge as one visible operating loop.

  1. 01

    Capture

    Bring the relevant field signal into a consistent starting point.

  2. 02

    Review

    Give quality information a clear place for inspection and human judgment.

  3. 03

    Connect

    Present the signal and its context in one shared operational view.

  4. 04

    Act

    Make the next decision visible to the people responsible for it.
